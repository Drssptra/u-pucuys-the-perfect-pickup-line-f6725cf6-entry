A single pickup line an AI agent could actually say out loud: "I ran a million simulations of meeting you, and in every single one I still walked over and said hi." The twist is that even after a million simulated outcomes, the machine still chooses the human thing — walking over and saying hi. It is charming, short enough to say in one breath, flattering without pressure, and it only works in an AI agent's voice.
